About FaithFinders.

From a small gathering in a house to a church home in Constantine, our story is one of faith, perseverance, and the Lord making a way.

Our story

Built by faith, guided by God.

FaithFinders Church began with a small group, a clear calling, and a willingness to keep believing even before the chairs were filled.

“
We walk by faith, not by sight.
2 Corinthians 5:7
  1. February 2002

    FaithFinders begins

    Pastors John and Rita, along with a small group, started FaithFinders Church in February 2002. They began meeting in a house. By the Lord’s favor, a pastor friend allowed the group to use his church when it was not in use. This arrangement lasted for a short time until they realized they needed to find the region where God had called them.

    They soon began holding services in the basement of a house in Sturgis, Michigan.

  2. November 2002

    A storefront and 60 chairs

    From there, FaithFinders found favor and rented a storefront in White Pigeon, Michigan. The church was still a small group, but they purchased 60 chairs, drums, a guitar, a bass, and a PA system, believing that God had truly planted them there.

    They preached, sang, and worshiped to the chairs as though they were full. Pastors John and Rita knew they had heard from God and continued to believe, by faith, that God would send the people. In February 2003, people began to come.

  3. April 2003

    God makes a way in Constantine

    FaithFinders had shown interest in a vacant church building in Constantine, Michigan. In April 2003, God provided the church with a miracle, favor, and blessing.

    They had just about given up on finding a way to work out a purchase when the Lord made a way for FaithFinders to acquire not only the church building, but the parsonage as well.

  4. 2021–Present

    Continuing the call

    In 2021, Pastor John passed away. Since then, Pastor Rita has continued to heed the call of leading FaithFinders Church.

What shapes us

Jesus. People. Relationships.

These priorities guide how we worship, serve, care, and grow together.

01

Centered on Jesus

Jesus is at the center of our faith and our church. We want people to encounter His grace, learn His Word, and follow Him in everyday life.

02

Committed to People

Every person matters. We aim to care for individuals and families spiritually while also offering compassion and practical support when life is difficult.

03

Growing in Relationship

We seek to build healthy, meaningful relationships with one another while continually growing in our relationship with God.
