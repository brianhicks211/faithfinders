The heart of FaithFinders

Jesus. People. Relationships.

These three priorities shape the kind of church community we are building together.

01

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus is the Son of God who came and gave His life for our sins. Our hope is that every person will come to know Him personally.

02

People

We care about you and your family. Our desire is to offer spiritual encouragement while also helping people through the realities of everyday life.

03

Relationships

We cultivate meaningful relationships with one another while growing together in our relationship with God.
