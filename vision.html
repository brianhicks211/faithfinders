Believing beyond what we can see

Our Vision.

A vision formed through trials, tested faith, and a belief that God is still calling His people to worship freely, serve boldly, and live as one body.

A word from Pastor John

Formed through trials and testing.

Through all of the trials and testing of faith that Pastor Rita and I have gone through, God has used it to form a vision for FaithFinders.

We can be considered different in how we believe and conduct a service. We may seem unstructured or too free. We could also be labeled as strange in the eyes of mainstream denominational churches. However, what we believe can be found in the same Bible that all Christian churches are reading.

Worship Freely.

Freedom in worship

Many expressions. One focus.

We may dance with taborets, run, jump, leap, or have faith marches. People may speak in other tongues or shout, and I may carry a rod or staff around the church. Our music may be upbeat and contemporary in style.

During worship, you may see men and women lying face down on the floor, standing, crying, or raising their arms. We believe God to be the same yesterday, today, and forever. If it was good for the saints of old, why would it not be good for us today?

Psalms 23:4; 33:3; 40:3; 63:4; 134:2; 149; 150 · Matthew 11:17; 28:4 · Mark 6:13; 16:18 · John 18:16 · Acts 9:4; 22:7; 28:8 · 1 Corinthians 14:15 · 1 Timothy 2:8 · James 5:14 · Revelation 1:17 · 2 Chronicles 5:14 · Lamentations 2:19

The Body of Christ

Connected as one family.

We believe that the Body of Christ should be just what it says: “The Body of Christ.” The body is supposed to be connected. We are to be a family.

Just think what could happen in our communities if Christians fellowshipped publicly and proclaimed that we are all believers who simply attend different buildings for worship. Wouldn’t unbelievers become curious?

We believe God’s vision is to unite Christians into one body. A church can have people leaping and running while others sit and meditate on the Lord—each person focused on God rather than on how someone else is praising. Everyone should be allowed to praise and worship in their own way, with the only exception being not to interfere with someone else’s worship. We successfully do this now, and it can work.

The vision in action

Faith that reaches everyday life.

Our vision extends beyond a weekly gathering and into the spiritual, practical, and relational needs of people and the community.

01

Community Involvement

We envision a community-oriented church that participates in town functions, parades, Christmas events, support for people in need, and other opportunities to serve.

We want believers who are not afraid to work side by side with people who are not saved, using service and everyday activities to witness to the unchurched rather than hiding inside a building.

02

Faith School

We envision a K–12 Christian school that teaches reading, writing, and arithmetic alongside Christian faith, morals, and values.

Built on biblical principles, the school would educate children and help mold them into tomorrow’s leaders.

03

Faith Housing Program

We envision a program that purchases and remodels homes, then places a faithful, tithing Christian family in each home for a one- to two-year probationary period.

During that time, the family would learn how to manage and maintain a home, after which they may have the opportunity to purchase it outright.

04

Youth Ministry

We envision an extraordinary youth program that captures the attention of young people throughout the area and meets them at their level.

The program would instill biblical principles that remain useful when life’s toughest decisions arise.

05

Men’s & Women’s Groups

We envision groups that uplift brothers and sisters through life crises, daily struggles, failures, addictions, and every fiery dart that can keep people from living productive Christian lives.

06

Children’s Church

We envision a children’s church where parents can worship while their children are well cared for and learning about Jesus.

Children would not only hear stories about biblical people, but understand the values and concepts God intended them to know.

1,500

A God-sized future

A 1,500-seat sanctuary and a membership of 5,000.

We do not know how or where this will come to pass, but we do know that with God all things are possible.

Matthew 19:26

The invitation

Will you help?

Rita and I have a big vision that will take an army of believers to fulfill. It will take dedicated believers who are in the church to work for the Kingdom of God—people who will work long, hard hours and receive little to no praise for what they do.

It will take people who know they are called to work in the Kingdom, accept their gifts and callings for what they are, and use them to the utmost degree.

We will see this vision come to pass. The question is, “Will you help?”

— Pastor John
